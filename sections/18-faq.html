What is AI governance software?

AI governance software sits between employees and every model, agent, and company system, enforcing identity, data protection, policy, model routing, cost caps, and audit logging on each request. AI Guardian delivers this as enterprise AI governance software plus a governed employee workplace in Teams, Slack, web, and mobile, going further than typical AI gateway software for enterprise developer teams by adding the business-facing controls IT, Risk, and Finance need.
