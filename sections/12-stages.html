Adopt it in stages

Start with shadow AI. Expand the same platform when you are ready.

The scope of governance widens over time, but it is the same control plane at every stage, so nothing gets re-bought, re-integrated, or re-governed as you extend it.

  1. Start here

    Control shadow AI

    Give every employee one approved way to use AI.

    Gain enterprise-scale visibility, control, and cost management over usage from day one.

    Replace personal-account AI use with a governed alternative.

  2. As you build

    Govern your own agents

    Agents built in Foundry or Copilot Studio register with AI Guardian.

    They run behind the same control plane without a line of their code changing.

    Identity, guardrails, and audit trail apply automatically.

  3. As you extend

    Govern bought and BYO agents

    Knowledge agents grounded in your own content join the same registry.

    Third-party agents that act inside your existing tools through MCP are governed the same way.

    No separate policy layer for purchased tools.

  4. At full scope

    The platform for all AI

    Workflows, future agents, and knowledge systems plug into the same plane.

    One policy model, one audit trail, and one cost view across every AI system in the business.

    Whatever comes next runs under the same enterprise AI governance platform.
